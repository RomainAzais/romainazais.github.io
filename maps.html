Maps

Soft mobility across Greater Lyon

This interactive map shows soft mobility across Greater Lyon through purpose-built measures of the TCL public transport network, the TER regional rail network and the Vélo'v bike-sharing system. Each measure is shown throughout the day, in 15-minute slots, for weekdays, Saturdays and Sundays. It is based on theoretical timetables for TCL (Oct 2025 – Jan 2026) and TER (Sep 2026 – Feb 2027), and historical Vélo'v usage data (Jan 2026 – Jun 2026).

Private rents across Greater Lyon

This interactive map shows how private-sector rents evolved across Greater Lyon between 2014 and 2024 (2025 data are not yet available). The data come from the Observatoire Local des Loyers and have been mapped onto the 512 IRIS (Îlots Regroupés pour l'Information Statistique) of the metropolitan area
